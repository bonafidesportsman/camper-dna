Kit & Gear Accessories

Accessories

The bits that don't make it into the main spec conversation but matter enormously day-to-day. Awnings, window screens, and a proper mattress topper are the three things most people wish they'd sorted from the start.

Awnings

There are two fundamentally different products here. A retractable awning mounts permanently to the van's roof rail and rolls out to give you a 2–3 metre canopy – shade from the sun, light rain cover, somewhere to sit outside without sitting in the road. The Fiamma F45S is the default choice for T5 and T6: it fits the factory rail without drilling, rolls up tidily, and is available in several widths. About £490 fitted.

An awning rail is a different thing entirely: an aluminium track bonded to the van's side that lets you attach a drive-away tent. The tent stays pitched when you drive off — useful for holding your spot and keeping gear when you head off for trips away from the campsite — and clips back in when you return. Kiravans make a T5/T6 kit, much cheaper than a full awning, with more versatile space for families, but it's a tent, not a quick-deploy canopy.

Prices shown as a guide — they can change at any time.

The mattress topper you shouldn't skip

Conversion beds are almost universally built on 60–75mm foam boards. They're fine for a night. After three nights you'll feel it. The RIB Altair bed system suits a variety of toppers (Duvalay is popular) but the Edge Campervans RIB112 folds with the bed and makes the sleeping surface genuinely comfortable. At approx. £140 it's the difference between "camping sleep" and actual sleep. The advantage of this topper is that it folds neatly onto the rear shelf when not in use and doesn't take up much space.

Always check compatibility with your specific vehicle before buying. Van models, years, and conversion specs vary — we can't guarantee every product fits every setup. If in doubt, contact the manufacturer or your converter.

Window screens

Custom-fit window screens that clip to the van's door rubbers from inside. They black out completely, insulate better than glass, and take about 90 seconds to fit. You can buy cheaper thin versions but Rainbow Screens make thermal screens that keep the heat in and fit the exact window shapes for different van models. At around £220 they're more premium but we've had ours for 6 years and counting.
