Kit & Gear Accessories

Accessories

The bits that don't make it into the main spec conversation but matter enormously day-to-day. Awnings, window screens, and a proper mattress topper are the three things most people wish they'd sorted from the start.

Awnings

There are two fundamentally different products here. A retractable awning mounts permanently to the van's roof rail and rolls out to give you a 2–3 metre canopy — shade from the sun, light rain cover, somewhere to sit outside without sitting in the road. The Fiamma F45S is the default choice for T5 and T6: it fits the factory rail without drilling, rolls up tidily, and is available in several widths. About £490 fitted.

An awning rail is a different thing entirely — a aluminium track bonded to the van's side that lets you attach a drive-away tent. The tent stays pitched when you drive off (useful for families at a campsite), and clips back in when you return. Kiravans make the standard T5/T6 kit. Much cheaper than a full awning, more versatile for families, but it's a tent — not a quick-deploy canopy.

The mattress topper you shouldn't skip

Conversion beds are almost universally built on 60–75mm foam boards. They're fine for a night. After three nights you'll feel it. The RIB Altair bed system (the one most quality converters use) is designed to accept a specific topper — the Edge Campervans RIB112 — which folds with the bed and makes the sleeping surface genuinely comfortable. At £180 it's not cheap, but it's the difference between "camping sleep" and actual sleep.

Window screens

Custom-fit window screens that clip to the van's door rubbers from inside. They black out completely, insulate better than glass, and take about 90 seconds to fit. The go-to brand for T5 and T6 is Rainbow Screens — they make them to the exact window shape. At around £80 for a set, they're cheap for what they deliver. Currently not listed in our affiliate catalogue but worth sourcing direct from rainbowscreens.co.uk.
