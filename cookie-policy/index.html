Cookie Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device. We keep our use of them minimal — no cross-web tracking, no advertising profiles. What we do use is explained plainly below.

1. The Types of Cookies We Use

Essential cookies. Required for the site to work. They handle things like remembering your cookie preferences so we don't ask you every visit. You cannot opt out of essential cookies — without them, parts of the site won't function.

Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4). We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people use CamperDNA — which pages are popular, how people move through the questionnaire, and where visitors come from. All data collected is anonymised. We don't use it to identify individual visitors. These cookies are only set if you accept analytics cookies when the banner appears.

Advertising cookies. We do not currently use advertising cookies. If we ever introduce display advertising, we will update this policy and our consent banner before doing so, and ask for fresh consent at that point.

2. Cookie Reference

3. Managing Your Preferences

When you first visit CamperDNA, a banner will ask for your cookie preferences. You can accept all, accept essential only, or customise your choices. You can change your preferences at any time via the 'Cookie Settings' link in the site footer.

You can also manage or delete cookies directly through your browser:

  • Chrome — Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox — Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari — Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Edge — Settings > Cookies and site permissions

Blocking all cookies may affect how CamperDNA functions, particularly the questionnaire tool.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use (such as Google Analytics). We don't control how those third parties use the data collected. We'd encourage you to review Google's privacy policy for detail on how GA4 data is handled.

5. Changes to This Policy

We'll update this policy whenever we make meaningful changes to how we use cookies. The 'Last updated' date at the top will change when that happens.
